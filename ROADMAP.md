# Second pass

Ordered by what I think pays off most once you've played it. Phase 1 is polish on what's
there; phases 2–3 add features in small, testable slices.

## Phase 1: feel and readability (after your playtest)

1. **Throw readability.** Faint landing-spot ghost for the first day (fades out Tuesday), and a
   "sweet spot" glint on the road beside each upcoming subscriber so mailbox lanes are learnable.
2. **Hit-stop and juice.** 2–3 frame freeze plus a punch zoom on mailbox hits, window smashes and
   crashes; the paper lands *in* the mailbox (door flips shut).
3. **Rider reactions.** Glance back at a chasing dog, fist-pump on streak ×4, wave at porch
   neighbours, coast hands-free across the finish, dismount on the summary.
4. **Dog telegraph.** Crouch-wiggle before the lunge and a bark ring, so every dog crash feels fair.
5. **Camera.** Lean the framing toward the side you're throwing at; a touch of FOV at sprint.
6. **Audio layers.** Rolling noise by surface (asphalt, sidewalk, grass), wind at speed, drums
   that kick in on sprint, ambience per district (sprinklers, birdsong, construction).
7. **Difficulty pass** from real runs: subscriber density, dog counts per day, traffic gaps.
8. **Performance.** Build lot meshes in a worker so streaming never hitches; instance trees.

## Phase 2: content

- **Neighbours** on porches: catch papers (bonus), wave, or shake a fist when you miss.
- **Trash day Thursday** (garbage truck, cans everywhere), **sprinklers** that make you slide,
  **puddles** after Tuesday rain, **joggers and a skateboard kid** on the sidewalk.
- **Weather and light per day:** drizzle, fog, golden-hour Friday.
- **End-of-day BMX bonus course** (Paperboy 2's training ground): ramps, targets, a timer.
- **Rails and grinds**, with air + deliveries feeding one combo meter.

## Phase 3: longevity

- **Tips and upgrades** between days: bigger bag, better brakes, a bell that scares dogs.
- **Second route** ("Lakeside") unlocked by a Gold week; **rival paperboy** race on Fridays.
- **Gamepad and touch controls.**
- **High-score table with initials** and a **ghost** of your best run.
- **Photo mode** in the rider lab.
